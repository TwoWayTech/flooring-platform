# ADR 0037: Sales on the API, part 1 (customers, quotes and the tax engine)

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-14
- **Context:** the owner's second work queue, task 5: P11 sales with the tax engine (docs/product-spec/sales.md). Sales is the largest module, so it lands in two parts. This one covers customers, quotes and the tax engine. The next covers invoices (conversion, sent, void, credit notes), reservations with the cut or issue (inventory part 2), payments with the two-person check, write-offs, credits and refunds.

## Decision
- **Data scope** (spec "Data scope"): customers and quotes are read and changed only within the person's scope. Own is by rep; branch is by the quote's branch, and for a customer by the rep's branch; all is everything. Anything outside the scope answers 404.
- **Customers:**
  - **Adding:** needs only a name. The person becomes the rep, and the company's default terms apply.
  - **Editing:** changes the name, contact details, terms and addresses. New addresses get server ids, the defaults follow them, and removed addresses are soft-deleted.
  - **Fields never taken from a request:** credit limit, tax exemption, certificate and rep have no edit screen. The credit limit is stripped without `customers.customer.view_sensitive`.
- **Quotes:**
  - **What the server sets:** status, sent and expiry dates change only through `POST /quotes/:id/status`, using the shared `quoteActions` and `quoteSendProblems`. The rep and the number (taken under a lock) are the server's. The branch is always the warehouse's, re-derived whenever the warehouse changes, so data scope follows where the quote is fulfilled from.
  - **States:** a job-site or address state is trimmed, uppercased and must be a real US code. A send needs one. Tax rules match the code exactly, so a lowercase or made-up state would otherwise collect nothing (PR #14 security review).
  - **References:** each roll belongs to its line's product and is in stock; line warehouses and the sold-at location are live. Drawing rooms needs the Retail module and `retail.room.measure`.
  - **Totals:** computed on the server with the shared `computeTotals` and the tax engine (`taxContextFor` → `resolveTaxRule` → `withOverride`). The engine reads the company's own tax rules, county rates and company default, on today's date in the company's time zone. A total sent by the screen is ignored.
  - **Line cost, category and unit:** come from the catalog, never from the request (rule 5; the category decides tax). A line without a product keeps its saved category; adding one or changing its category needs `sales.tax.override`. Cost is stripped without `inventory.product.view_sensitive`.
  - **Moving the job site:** a quote with a tax override cannot change state or county without `sales.tax.override`. Sending prices the quote again on the day it goes out.
  - **Rights:** a waste change needs `sales.waste.view`. A tax change needs `sales.tax.override`, a valid rate and a reason; who changed it and when are recorded by the server, and the change is audited on its own.
  - **Rooms and lines:** replaced as a whole on each save, with line room links following the rooms' new ids.
  - **Email:** `quote.sent` emails the customer through NotificationService.
- **Settings:** a company with no localization settings yet answers 409 `settings_missing`. This fails closed: no quote is priced without its tax rules.
- **Seed:** Summit's customers with their addresses, contacts and certificates, and its quotes with their rooms and lines.
- **Web:**
  - The Sales and quote pages read through `salesData()`. The quote page reads its products, rolls and stock through `inventoryData()`, so its ids match the API.
  - `createCustomer`, `saveCustomer`, `saveQuote` and `updateQuoteStatus` are in API_METHODS.
  - Invoices stay on the demo until part 2.

## Accountant decisions (STATUS)
- **Where the company collects tax:** which states it is registered in, and its states of operation, stay company settings (Settings → Localization). The safe default collects only where a rule exists for the job site, or the company default in its states of operation.
- **Rates and taxability per state:** these are the accountant's to confirm. The seeded rules carry the demo's example note.

## A cost the server withheld, on the web (2026-11-16, STATUS B1)
- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-16, on branch `fix/sales-quote-without-cost` (PR #46, stacked on PR #45). It is the third bug of the owner's queue of 2026-11-16 (STATUS "Api-mode bugs the flows found", B1). ADR 0058 is reserved by the paused whole-product check, so this is a dated section of the sales ADR that set up quote stripping. Session log docs/sessions/2026-11-16-2320-b-quote-without-cost.md.
- **Context:** the API strips `cost` and `unitCost` for anyone without `inventory.product.view_sensitive` (CLAUDE.md rule 5; `SENSITIVE_FIELDS`), so in api mode a salesperson's products and quote lines arrive without them. The web's `lineFor` copied the missing `product.cost` into a new line, and `computeTotals` multiplied by it on every render. `new Decimal(undefined)` threw, and the quote builder showed "This screen stopped working." once a quote had a product line. Saved quotes, the save's answer and a status change's answer broke the same way. The server was already right: `trustedLines` costs every line from the catalog or the saved line and ignores a sent cost.

### Decisions
- **A withheld cost is unknown, never zero** (D1, D2). `computeTotals` takes `ViewedDocLine`, a `DocLine` whose `unitCost` may be absent or null. When any line lacks it, the document's `cost`, `margin` and `marginPct`, and that line's `cost`, are null: never zero (a 100 % margin) and never a sum of the known costs (an understated cost). Amounts, tax and total never depend on cost. One calculator with nullable outputs, so every consumer handles the unknown case at compile time and sales.md rule 13's single calculator stays true.
- **Stored lines keep a required cost** (D3, D4). `DocLine.unitCost` stays `string`. A document being stored takes its cost through `storedCost`, which throws "A document line has no cost, so the document can't be stored." on null. It is used at the four places that store a cost: `invoiceFromQuote`, `invoiceFromJob`, the fixtures' `documentAmounts` and the business-report gate test. None can meet a null today (the API reads `unit_cost NOT NULL` rows or `trustedLines`' lines; DemoStore's lines always carry cost), and throwing beats storing `0`.
- **A new line never fills in a cost** (D7). `lineFor` moved to `packages/shared/src/operations/quotes.ts` as the exported `quoteLineFor`, unit-tested: a product without `cost` gives a line whose JSON has no `unitCost`. The client never sends a cost it doesn't hold, and the server ignores one anyway. Its two identical `measuredFromRooms` branches collapsed into one.
- **A quote's margin needs both keys and a known cost** (D5, D6). `seesQuoteMargin` (shared, next to `SENSITIVE_FIELDS`, CLAUDE.md rule 4) needs `sales.margin.view_sensitive` and `inventory.product.view_sensitive`, because margin is the subtotal less cost and gives the cost away, as the reports' margin flag already says (ADR 0055 S17). `QuoteSummary` shows the Margin row, with Cost under it, only with both keys and a non-null cost; it is hidden, with no "not available" note, as every sensitive field is. In api mode the null check alone hides it, since the server withholds cost; the key rule changes only the demo, where DemoStore sends cost to every browser, and makes the two modes agree. No default role changes; a custom role with the margin key but not product cost loses a quote's margin in the demo.
- **No server change.** An API test pins the contract the client relies on (D13): a line sent without `unitCost` is costed from the catalog, the rep's answers never carry cost, the manager's do, and a cost sent with a save is ignored.
- **DemoStore is unchanged** (D8). It never lacks cost (sales.md known gaps), so the demo can't exercise a withheld cost; its e2e run is regression only.
- **F1 runs again** (D11, D12), with rule 5 checked the other way (Priya's quote request and its answer carry no `unitCost`; her summary shows no margin) and three clean-ups (void the invoice, void the deposit, reject the final payment while it waits). None voids a confirmed final payment, which would post entries after a passing run.

### The audit: every place a withheld cost meets decimal maths
- **Fixed here:** the quote builder (new lines, saved quotes, the save's and status change's answers) and its summary. `quoteLineFor` is the only place the web builds a document line; invoices are built on the server.
- **Safe:** the invoice page and lines, the payment sheet, credit notes, contracts (`estimatedCost` is optional and its input is behind `seesCost`), roll quick invoices, and the reports, which are worked out on the server in api mode.
- **B5, recorded, not fixed:** a salesperson's dashboard (her landing page) shows "This page could not load its data." in api mode, because `GET /invoices` withholds invoice `cost` and `monthlyPnl`, `salesTotals`, `bestSellersIn` and the margin sparkline add or subtract it. Sales → Team's `teamStats` reads it too, but loads today only because its people still come from DemoStore, whose ids match no API invoice. The fix needs this section's unknown-cost rule and the both-keys rule for the margin displays gated by the margin key alone, and it changes `monthlyPnl`, which feeds the API's P&L, so it has its own PR and security review. Its test is committed as `fixme` in `apps/web/tests/e2e-api/sales.spec.ts`. (Fixed 2026-11-17, see below.)
- **Listed for later:** the cost displays gated only by the shell's `can()` (`product-drawer.tsx`, `roll/roll-page.tsx`, `products-table.tsx`, `rolls-table.tsx`, `purchasing/order-drawer.tsx`) crash in `formatNumber(undefined)` only when the shell's permissions say yes while the server stripped the cost, which no default role reaches; they go with the `GET /company` move (D19). The installation jobs move must handle a withheld `jobCost`, `laborCost` and `jobMargin`.
- **Not B1:** O2, the payment sheet's "Status after" preview (a different screen and rule).

### Follow-ups
- Typed "as received" records, so the contract types say which fields may be withheld (`Product.cost`, `DocLine.unitCost`, `Invoice.cost`, `ContractLine.estimatedCost`, `Employee.payRate`).
- DemoStore stripping cost like the API, so the demo meets the same paths.
- The View as banner's wording for a margin-only role, after B5 (D18). (Done 2026-11-17, see below.)
- B6, found while testing: a saved quote can't be saved again in api mode (the API answers percents at `numeric(18,8)` scale, which its own percent check refuses, and its waste check compares text). Its own PR with a security review.

## A withheld cost on the dashboard and Sales → Team (2026-11-17, STATUS B5)
- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-17, on branch `fix/sales-dashboard-without-cost` (PR #48, stacked on PR #47, which is stacked on #46 and #45). It is B5 of the owner's queue of 2026-11-16, found in B1's audit above. Session log docs/sessions/2026-11-17-0146-b-dashboard-without-cost.md.
- **Context:** `GET /invoices` leaves out an invoice's `cost` and every line's `unitCost` for anyone without `inventory.product.view_sensitive` (`SENSITIVE_FIELDS`), and a contract invoice's also for anyone without `contracts.contract.view_sensitive` (`contractCostShown`). The dashboard's loader then ran `monthlyPnl` over a salesperson's invoices, `dec(undefined)` threw, and her landing page showed "This page could not load its data.". Behind the loader, `salesTotals` (the figure cards), `bestSellersIn` (Best sellers), the margin sparkline and `teamStats` (Sales → Team, the top performer) did the same maths. Separately, every margin on these screens was gated by `sales.margin.view_sensitive` alone, so in the demo, where DemoStore sends cost to every browser, a margin-only custom role read the cost through the margin.

### Decisions
- **Unknown is never zero or a partial sum, per set** (D1). The set is what a figure adds up: a month (`monthlyPnl`) and the 12 months together (`pnlTotals`); a period (`salesTotals`, `marginSeries`); the Best sellers window with its filters, all products together, because rows rank against each other (`bestSellersIn`); a salesperson's issued invoices (`teamStats`). Zero would show a 100 % margin, and a partial sum would understate cost.
- **One calculation per figure, with nullable outputs** (D2). `ViewedInvoice` (types/sales.ts) is an invoice as its viewer holds it: `cost` and the lines' `unitCost` may be absent or null. `Invoice` stays the stored shape and is assignable to it. `PnlMonth.cogs`, `grossMargin`, `grossMarginPct` and `netIncome`, `SalesTotals.cost`, `margin` and `marginPct`, `SellerRow.margin` and `TeamRow.margin` are nullable, so every consumer handles the unknown case at compile time. Revenue, quantity, expenses, paid revenue and commission never depend on cost.
- **The rule lives once** (D19). `sumKnown` (money.ts) sums values that may be unknown: one null makes the sum null, and an empty list is a known zero. `signedSubtotal` and `signedCost` are exported from analytics.ts, and `signedCost` gives null for a withheld cost. `monthlyPnl`, `salesTotals`, `marginSeries`, `teamStats` and B1's `computeTotals` use them; `bestSellersIn` keeps each product's margin as known or unknown and never multiplies a missing cost as zero.
- **Stored data keeps a required cost** (D3). `storedPnl` returns the months with every figure, or throws "An invoice has no cost, so the profit and loss can't be worked out." `pnlReport`, the fixture generator and the demo e2e's expected figures read through it, as stored documents read through `storedCost`. Their inputs can't hold a null (`invoices.cost NOT NULL`; DemoStore's rows), and throwing beats printing a partial cost of goods, whatever the report's margin flag says.
- **A display shows margin only with both keys and every value it shows known, all or nothing within the display** (D4). The 12-month panel's title, two key figures, chart line and table columns go together (`pnlTotals` gives margin for all twelve months or none; `knownPnl` gives the months); the Gross margin card needs its value and its sparkline (`marginSeries`); Best sellers' Margin option and column go together; Sales → Team's Margin column, its total and the CSV need every shown row known.
- **One two-key rule** (D5). `seesQuoteMargin` is renamed `seesMargin`, and `reportVisibility`'s margin flag calls it with an identical truth table. `FigureRow`, `RevenuePanel`, `BestSellers`, `TeamTable` and `QuoteSummary` use it. No default role changes in a company with its modules on.
- **The dashboard doesn't copy the reports' company-level contract fact** (D6). The browser sees only its own invoices, and a contract invoice the viewer can't cost arrives without cost, so D1 hides margin for every month, period or salesperson whose set holds one. Best sellers is never affected: a contract invoice's lines carry no product, so `bestSellersIn` never counts them.
- **Ranking by an unknown margin falls back to revenue** (D7), in shared, and the screen doesn't offer Margin. The screen also ranks by revenue whenever the viewer may not see margin, so the bars' order always matches what they show (for example after View as).
- **The margin card leaves its change line out when the earlier period's margin is unknown** (D8): "No earlier period to compare" would be untrue. Only custom roles and Contracts-off companies reach it (owner question Q5).
- **No API change** (D9). The server is right. A new `invoices.int.test.ts` case pins what the web relies on (a salesperson's `GET /invoices` has no `cost` or `unitCost`; the Owner's has both).
- **DemoStore is unchanged** (D10), so the demo still sends cost to every browser (owner question Q3).
- **The View as banner says "Costs are hidden."** for a role with the margin key but not product cost (D11, B1 D18). It says nothing about margins, because the server's commission margin bases still need only the margin key (ADR 0048; owner question Q2).
- **`FigureRow` and `hiddenNote` are exported, not moved** (D12), in a behaviour-neutral edit before the red run, so the unit tests reach them.
- **`pnlTotals` holds the 12-month totals in shared** (D18). The hook (`useDashboardData`) holds no maths and reads no permissions; `RevenuePanel` decides with `seesMargin`, so View as keeps working.

### The P&L on the server is unchanged
`pnlReport`'s only change is reading its months through `storedPnl`, which only throws, and only on a null that the API's report rows (`invoices.cost NOT NULL`) and DemoStore's rows can't hold. No file under `apps/api/src` or `packages/db` changes. The proof: S-HUB-1's golden totals, R-EQ, R-STRIP-1, -3 and -6 and R-MODULES-OFF (`reports.int.test.ts`, the same before and after), `report-visibility.test.ts` unchanged, and the fixed-seed regeneration through the edited fixture generator.

### Parity between the demo and api mode
- **The same for every default role in a company with its modules on.** Every margin on the dashboard, Best sellers and Sales → Team needs both keys. In api mode the server's stripping already hid it from anyone without product cost, and the null rule keeps the page standing; in the demo the key rule now does the same. A margin-only custom role sees no margin in either mode. The calculations are one shared code path; only their inputs differ.
- **Still different:** a viewer with the margin key and product cost but without the effective `contracts.contract.view_sensitive`, in a company with contract invoices: a custom role, or any role, the Owner included, in a company with Contracts off. In api mode the server strips those invoices' cost, and the null rule hides margin for every month, period and salesperson whose set holds one (before B5 such an Owner's dashboard crashed too). In the demo DemoStore doesn't strip, so those margins show, with the contract invoices' estimated cost included. Best sellers is the same in both modes. Closed by DemoStore stripping invoice cost the way the API does (Q3).

### Follow-ups
- Sales → Team's api-mode e2e comes back with `GET /company`, once users come from the API (its people are DemoStore's today, whose ids match no API invoice).
- Typed "as received" records, now with two viewed types (`ViewedDocLine`, `ViewedInvoice`); `SalesLedger.invoices` is still typed `Invoice[]`.
- The job card's "Materials at cost" and "Job margin" (`features/installation/job-card.tsx`) need only `installation.job.view_sensitive`; once Installation moves to the API they need the both-keys and unknown-cost rules.
- The revenue chart's tooltip passes the chart's float to `<Num>` (pre-existing; rule 6): carry the decimal strings under their own keys.
- Commission margin bases (HR → Commissions, the portal) and the access preview's "Sales margin" for a margin-only role (owner question Q2).

## A write takes back what the API answered (2026-11-17, STATUS B6)
- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-17, on branch `fix/sales-quote-resave` (PR #49, stacked on PR #48, which is stacked on #47, #46 and #45). STATUS "Api-mode bugs the flows found", B6, found by B1's API test; PR #46 Q6. ADR 0058 is reserved, so this is a dated section of the sales part 1 ADR, which holds the waste right (Decision, "Rights"). It also covers the contract's retainage check (contracts: ADR 0044) and the commission split's share check (the route: ADR 0043), so look for those here, not there. It closes the B6 follow-up above. Session log docs/sessions/2026-11-17-0300-b-quote-resave.md.
- **Context:** percents are numeric(18,8) and answered at that scale (`"50.00000000"`); the quote's check took four decimals, and the waste check compared text. Sending the answer back was 400 for anyone; sending typed percents was 403 for a salesperson, so no client could save a saved quote again. A draft contract's retainage and an invoice's commission share were refused the same way. A malformed or empty percent answered 500 (Zod 4 runs the bound after a failed format, and `dec("abc")` throws).

### Decisions
- **The server takes back what it answers, not the client** (D1). A client-only fix can't work: the answer's text fails the format (400) and typed text fails the waste check (403). It would also need every client and screen (quote builder, contract form, split dialog) to know each column's scale.
- **Spellings, not values** (D2). A percent may be written with up to eight decimals, the column's scale, but the places past the rule (four for a quote's deposit and waste and a contract's retainage, two for a commission share) may only be zeros: `PERCENT_TEXT` and `SHARE_PERCENT_TEXT` in `packages/shared/src/decimal-text.ts`. The set of accepted values is unchanged. Rejected: any eight decimals (more values without an owner decision) and normalising inside the schema (numeric stores the value either way).
- **Answers are unchanged** (D3). api-contract "Bodies" already says decimal text is not canonical and clients compare by value. Short answers would change the wire of quotes, invoices, contracts, splits and reports for every reader; the inputs showing `50.00000000` in api mode are an owner question (Q2).
- **Waste is compared by value and fails closed** (D4). `assertWaste` uses the shared `sameOptionalDecimal`: `"10"` and `"10.00000000"` are the same waste, a blank and a number are not, and the smallest real change (`0.0001`) still needs `sales.waste.view`. Key holders skip the check as before.
- **Only plain decimal text can match** (D14). decimal.js alone reads `"0x0a"`, `"1e1"`, `"+10"` and `"10."` as 10, so `sameOptionalDecimal` checks `/^\d+(\.\d+)?$/` on both sides and answers "changed" otherwise: a permission check built on it gives 403, never 500, and never "same" for odd text.
- **A saved line is the same line only with the same id and product** (D13). B6 lets a salesperson's re-save of a line with waste through; the answer gives her every line's id, product and waste, so without this a tile line sent under a carpet line's id with `12.00000000` would be stored without the key. A line whose product differs from the saved line under its id is new and is compared with its product's default, in the API and in DemoStore. No screen changes a line's product in place. One shared helper, `savedQuoteLine`, decides which saved line a sent line is, for the API's `trustedLines` and `assertWaste` and the demo's `wasteChanged` (review round 1). So a line sent without the product it had is a new line without a product: it needs `sales.tax.override` and is costed at 0 like any new one, and keeps neither the saved category and cost nor the saved waste. With `trustedLines` still matching by id only, a salesperson's product line sent without its product and waste was stored (200), where the base refused it (403).
- **Each line is sent once** (review round 1). `POST` and `PUT /quotes` refuse repeated line ids (400 "Send each line once."). B6 lets a salesperson send back a waste a key holder set, so without this she could send that line twice and store the same waste on a second line, which as a new line must start from the default. DemoStore doesn't check it: no screen can send a line twice (the builder keys its lines by id), and the demo is not a boundary.
- **A bad format stops the check** (D5). The quote's and the contract's `PERCENT` regex has `abort: true`, so `"abc"` and `""` are 400 with the format message and the bound never reads text decimal.js can't. The share check has no bound after it.
- **The same class, fixed here** (D6). `PUT /contracts/:id` (the contract's and each line's `retainagePct` and `storedRetainagePct`) and `PUT /invoices/:id/commission-split` (the share) take back their own answers the same way; the split's sum, positive and "the rep keeps half" checks were already by value, so mixed scales (`"60.00000000"` with `"40"`) are fine.
- **DemoStore matches the comparison only** (D7). `wasteChanged` compares by value through the same helper and matches a saved line by id and product. Its blank rules stay looser than the API's (a line without waste is no change; a saved blank reads as `"0"`). It also reads the same text as no change before it compares by value (`wasteDiffers`, review round 2): DemoStore keeps waste as typed, and the line editor and the Settings default only drop characters other than digits and points, so it can hold `"12."` or `".5"`, which the shared helper never matches. Without this, a salesperson's re-save of a `"12."` a manager typed, or any save of a line at a `"12."` category default, was refused. The helper stays strict, and the API needs no such rule: `PERCENT_TEXT` refuses a bare point.
- **No delta row** (D9). No feature, permission, route or blueprint rule changes: the blueprint's numeric(18,8) rates and decimal strings stand, and the waste key rule is the same. D13 says what "the line had" means, a spec detail recorded in sales.md rule 8 and owner question Q6. No schema, migration, guard, tenancy or audit change.

### The sweep
- **Fixed:** the quote's deposit and waste, the contract's retainage (contract and lines) and the commission share, the three writes whose check refused the API's own answer.
- **Checked, safe:** every other write takes back what it answers. Quantities, prices and money are checked at their column's scale; the tax override, rooms and lines without a product round-trip as text; invoice and billing rates are never taken back; commission plan rates and time-off hours are answered short; timesheets and pay rates already compare by value. Settings has no API write yet.
- **Listed, not fixed:** the split dialog's float maths and its share pick by position (a re-save sends the rep twice when she holds the smaller share, api mode only); the recurring expense amount's 500 on malformed text; `wastePct` not stripped though sales.md says it must be; a time-off accrual rate below 1e-7 answered as `"5e-8"`, which can't be saved again.

### Follow-ups
- The inputs show percents at stored scale in api mode (Deposit percent, waste, Retainage %, the split share): short answers or `dec(x).toFixed()` in the inputs (Q2). Either way `toFixed()`, never `toString()`, which writes values below 1e-7 in exponent form. The same inputs keep a bare point as typed (`"12."`): the API answers 400 "Enter a percent such as 10.", and DemoStore stores it. Writing the value back on blur would fix both (review round 2).
- The split dialog: choose the shares by `userId === invoice.salesRepId` and work out the other share with decimal.js (rule 6), with PR #45's `Number(...)` sweep (Q4).
- The recurring expense amount's check needs `abort: true` (Q4).
- Whether waste is sensitive (Q3); if it is stripped, the waste check must keep a saved line's waste when it comes back without one.
- The Settings move: tax rule and county `ratePct` are numeric(18,8) and answered raw, so their write checks use the shared pattern.
- One waste rule for both modes in shared, once the demo's looser blank rules are settled.
