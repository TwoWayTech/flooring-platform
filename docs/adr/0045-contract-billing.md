# ADR 0045: Contract billings, retainage releases and their postings (contracts part 2)

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-14
- **Context:** contracts.md rules 4 to 19 describe progress billings and retainage releases, each raising one invoice (customer contract) or one bill (subcontract) with retainage held off its total. ADR 0044 put the register on the API and left billings read only. ADR 0039 left retainage to the contracts module: `invoicePosting` refuses an invoice that carries it. Every financial event posts a balanced entry through LedgerService (CLAUDE.md rule 10).

## Decision
- **Routes** (module `contracts`, switches `contracts.register` and `contracts.billing`, 404 when off; data scope through `lockContract`, 404 outside it):
  - `POST /contracts/:id/billings` (`contracts.billing.create`): `{ periodEnd, lines: [{ contractLineId, completedToDate, storedBalance }] }`. An active or completed project only. Rates are the line's override or the contract's, taken on the server and snapshotted per line (rule 4; fixes edge case 16). `billingProblem` must pass; each line appears once. Number is the count of the contract's billings plus one (rule 8), under the contract's row lock.
  - `POST /contracts/:id/retainage-releases` (`contracts.billing.create`): `{ amount }`, above zero and no more than held; `allocateRelease` shares it over the lines. No status guard, so retainage on a terminated or closed contract can still be paid out (edge case 4).
  - `POST /contract-billings/:id/void` (`contracts.billing.void`): `{ reason }` of 3 or more characters, the contract's highest-numbered posted billing only, not on a closed contract.
- **Documents:** a customer billing raises `contractInvoice` (tax from the company's rules for the job site on the company's day, the customer exempt only with a valid certificate, cost share per line from the sensitive estimated cost), numbered with the company's invoice prefix. A subcontract billing raises `contractBill` (number `<contract>-<n>`, no tax, no order or receipts). The billing is written first, then its document, then the billing's link to it, all in one transaction with the posting and the audit entries. Invoices go through the shared writer `apps/api/src/modules/sales/invoice-writes.ts` (`takeInvoiceNumber`, `insertInvoice`), which converting a quote and the roll invoice use too.
- **Lock order:** contract row, then the customer's lock, then the invoice-number lock, then the ledger. Billings touch no stock, so they take no stock lock. A void takes the contract row, the billing row, then the customer's lock or the bill row (`FOR UPDATE`, as supplier payments and expense voids take it), then the ledger.
- **Postings** (`packages/shared/src/accounting/contract-postings.ts`, event `contract_billing.posted`, source type `contract_billing`, source id the billing):
  - Customer invoice: debit Accounts Receivable for the total and Retainage Receivable for what is withheld; credit Sales for the gross at the contract's location and Sales Tax Payable for the tax. A release debits Accounts Receivable and credits Retainage Receivable for the released amount, plus Sales Tax Payable for any tax the treatment puts on it (rule 13).
  - Subcontract bill: debit Cost of Goods Sold for the gross at the contract's location; credit Accounts Payable for the total and Retainage Payable for what is withheld. A release debits Retainage Payable and credits Accounts Payable.
  - A negative amount (a lower cumulative figure on a line) moves to the other side, so every entry balances. Two default accounts are added: `retainage_receivable` (1150, asset) and `retainage_payable` (2150, liability); LedgerService makes them on first use in existing companies.
- **Voids** reverse the billing's entry on the void day (`LedgerService.reverse`). On a customer billing the invoice gets `voidedAt` and `voidReason`, refused while a payment is confirmed, credit or a write-off was applied, or a credit note is live; its pending payments are voided with it, as any invoice void does. On a subcontract billing the bill is soft-deleted, refused while a pending or confirmed supplier payment is allocated to it. The billing becomes `void` and its number is not reused.
- **Elsewhere:** `POST /invoices/:id/void` refuses a contract billing invoice ("void the billing instead", fixing edge case 11), and a credit note on one is refused (correct it on the next billing). Payables posts only the bills `enterBill` makes from an order's receipts, so a contract's bill never posts twice (ADR 0042). Commission treats contract invoices as any invoice (rule 16).
- **Refused:** a progress billing whose gross comes to less than zero (a negative invoice is a credit note's job); a master agreement's billing or release.
- **Notifications:** a customer billing or release emails `milestone.invoiced` to the customer after commit; a failure to queue never undoes the billing.
- **Audit:** `Posted contract billing`, `Released retainage` and `Voided contract billing` on the contract, plus `Created invoice for a contract billing` or `Entered bill for a contract billing` on the document. Customer entries carry gross, withheld or released; subcontract entries carry no money, as other supplier money stays out of the audit log. No estimated cost or cost share is ever audited.
- **Seed:** Summit's billings with their lines and releases, the contract invoices with their payments, the subcontract bill and any supplier payment that pays one, and the waiver on a contract-invoice payment (sm-lwv-0001). Seeded history posts no journal entry.
- **Web:** `postBilling`, `releaseRetainage` and `voidBilling` go to the API in api mode; the form's rates are not sent.

## Consequences
- Subcontracted work is booked to Cost of Goods Sold at the contract's location. The accountant may want a separate job-cost or subcontract-cost account; it is a key change in one posting rule.
- Retainage Receivable and Retainage Payable appear in the trial balance; the business report's balances still read invoice and bill ledgers, so held retainage stays outside Customers owe and You owe suppliers until released, as in the demo.
- Commission on a progress invoice still comes early by the retained amount (rule 16, plan K-10).
