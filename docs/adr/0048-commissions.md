# ADR 0048: Commissions on the API (plans, assignments, statements, adjustments) and how they reach payroll

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-14; amended 2026-11-15 with the owner's answers (below)
- **Context:** commissions.md describes plans with tiers, override, draw and clawback; assignments with a simulated signature; monthly statements derived from confirmed payments by one engine (`commissionLines`, `statementChain`); approval by HR; adjustments and reversal decisions; and payment through the next pay run (hr.md rule 19). The schema exists (`commission_plans`, `commission_assignments`, `commission_statements`, `commission_adjustments`; ADR 0025). ADR 0047 put pay runs on the API with commission at `0.00` and refused approval while an approved, unpaid statement waited. ADR 0043 put the invoice commission split on the API. Every financial event posts a balanced entry (CLAUDE.md rule 10); commission is pay (rule 5).

## Decision

### Routes, keys, switches and scope
- **HR → Commissions** (module `hr`, switch `hr.commissions`; keys exactly commissions.md "Who uses it"): `GET /commissions` (`hr.commission.view`) returns the statements of the last twelve months, every plan version, assignments, the company's members by name and invoice numbers in one answer (the screen used to read Users and Invoices, which HR can't); `POST /commission-statements/approve` and `POST /commission-reversals/decide` (`hr.commission.approve`); `POST /commission-adjustments` (`hr.commission.adjust`); `POST /commission-plans`, `PUT /commission-plans/:id` and `POST /commission-assignments` (`hr.commission.manage`). docs/api-contract.md, "Commissions".
- **Flooring Suite only:** commission is paid on sales, which live in `core`; the HR Suite has no Commissions page (commissions.md "Purpose"), so a company without `core` gets 404, as does a switched-off `hr.commissions`.
- **Company-wide:** every HR commission route needs data scope `all`, as payroll does (`requireCompanyWidePayroll`): commission is pay and the default holders (Owner, HR manager) are company-wide; a custom role at branch scope gets 403. The spec does not narrow `listCommissionStatements` by scope, which is only safe for company-wide viewers. A rep reads their own statements and signs their plan in the employee portal, which moves to the API in its own task.
- **Sales → Team:** `GET /commission-totals?from&to` (module `core`, switch `sales.team`, `sales.commission.view`): per person earned, paid and pending in the range (`commissionTotals`); data scope `all` sees everyone, anyone else themselves and the reps on the invoices their scope covers (commissions.md "Who uses it").
- **The commission split** stays where ADR 0043 put it (`PUT /invoices/:id/commission-split`).

### One engine
- The API loads plans, assignments, invoices (with cost, never sent on), payments, credit applied, the manager chain (user → linked employee → their manager on the line's confirmed day → that employee's user; "Overrides follow the manager on the line's day"), frozen months and decisions, and runs the shared `commissionLines`. Statement rows come from a new shared `commissionStatementRows`, which the demo's `statementRows` now calls too, so both build the same rows. `CommissionStatementRow` moved to packages/shared.
- **Contract invoices** are any invoice (commissions.md rule 19, ADR 0045): the progress invoice's subtotal, cost share and total feed `baseOf`; credit notes are netted by `netInvoice`; a release earns nothing.

### Statements
- **Approve** (`statementApprovalProblem`): the month has ended, the statement is open, no reversal waits for review, and every earlier month (in the twelve-month window) with commission or an adjustment is approved first, so a frozen month's carry is final. Under `PAYROLL_LOCK`, then the stored statement row (if any), then the rep's employee.
- **Approved means frozen** (commissions.md edge case "holds only in part", narrowed): adjustments and reversal decisions go only on months after the person's last approved one (`frozenThrough`), and a new assignment may start only after that month ends. The amount the approval posted is what the statement shows from then on and what the run pays (`postedCommission`); live figures can't drift it.
- **Paying:** a statement that pays something needs the rep's sign-in linked to a W-2 employee, because commission is paid only through a pay run; the expense goes to that employee's home location. Since 2026-11-15 approval also stores that employee on the statement (`commission_statements.employee_id`, whenever the rep is linked, even with nothing to pay), and the pay run pays that employee, not whoever the sign-in is linked to later ("The employee a statement pays").
- **Reversal decisions** check that the line is a reversal waiting for review on that very statement (closing the edge case "`decideReversal` does not check the line or month").
- **Adjustments:** `adjustmentProblem`; a deduction names a live invoice that person sold (as rep or in its split).
- **Plans:** `commissionPlanProblem` (name, rates 0 to 100 with up to four decimals, money with two, distinct tier points, a draw amount only with a draw, a clawback window of 0 to 3650 days). A new plan starts today as version 1. A plan is edited in place only while no approved or paid statement exists for a month from its start (safer than the demo's "a user on it has a non-draft statement", which missed managers paid overrides under it); otherwise a new version starts today, the old one retires yesterday, people on it move over unsigned, and an assignment starting later is repointed. Version, status and dates are the server's; the web sends only the terms.
- **Assignments:** `assignmentChanges` ends a running assignment the day before and replaces one starting on or after the new date (soft delete); a disabled or removed member can't be assigned; the plan must be active and already in force on that day.

### Separation of duties
- **Never one's own** (the Owner included, as for own pay facts and bonuses): nobody approves, adjusts or decides on their own statement, puts themselves on a plan, or changes a plan they are on. "Own" is the user, or a user whose linked employee is theirs by link or verified email (`selfCheck`).
- **Not what one shaped** (the Owner exempt, as at pay-run approval): nobody approves a statement they adjusted or decided a reversal on, or one whose month is covered by an assignment they made or whose plan (by assignment or by line) they added or changed (rows they created, or their append-only audit entries), or one with a line on a sale whose live commission split they set.
- **Linked deciders:** every write needs a sign-in linked to a live employee unless it is the Owner's (`requireLinkedDecider`).
- **Pay-run approval** keeps ADR 0047's rules; it no longer refuses waiting statements.

### Postings and payroll (`packages/shared/src/accounting/commission-postings.ts`)
- **Approval** (event `commission.approved`, source type `commission_statement`, source id the statement): debit **Commissions** (new default account `commissions_expense`, 6060, expense) at the rep's home location, credit **Accrued Payroll** for `payable`, dated the month's last day. Zero posts nothing. The memo is `Commission statement CS-<last 8 characters of its id>, <month>`: no name, no amount.
- **Reversals and adjustments** post nothing by themselves: commissions.md nets them into the open month's statement (rule 11), whose `payable` is never negative, so a clawback lowers a later approval and a shortfall carries forward. A posted approval is never reversed, because a statement never returns to draft.
- **The pay run** (ADR 0047) now pays approved statements whose `pay_period_id` is null or this run, for the people paid in it (by the employee the statement pays, `paidEmployee`): each pays the amount its approval posted, which joins `commissionPay` and gross pay, with the statements' lines allocated to their workweeks for the overtime true-up (`commissionAllocations`, hr.md rule 18). Approval links them (`pay_period_id`), a reopen releases them, an export marks them `paid`; each step is audited without an amount. Someone left out of the run keeps theirs waiting.
- **No double posting:** `payrollPosting` debits Wages for each line's gross pay minus its `commissionPay` (never below zero), because the commission is already in Accrued Payroll. Accrued Payroll therefore ends at the full gross the provider pays; Wages carries wages, bonuses and the overtime true-up, Commissions carries commission. A reopen reverses the payroll entry only; the commission stays accrued for the next approval.
- **Concurrency:** statement approvals, adjustments, decisions, plan saves and assignments take `PAYROLL_LOCK` first, as pay-run approval and reopen do, so a run never reads a statement half-approved and a statement never changes while being approved.

### Sensitive data and audit
- **Margin base:** on a gross margin plan a line's `base` is margin dollars (from the sensitive invoice cost), so `GET /commissions` sends it as `null` without `sales.margin.view_sensitive` (`hideMarginBase`), closing commissions.md edge case "Base is not masked for HR". The screen shows "Hidden". Invoice cost and margin percent are never sent.
- **No amounts in the log:** approval, adjustment (addition or deduction only), decisions, plans' terms, assignments (`ownTarget` true or false, never the figure) and pay-run steps.
- **Notification:** `commission.ready` to the rep after commit, restricted, with no amounts; a failure never undoes the approval.

### Audit visibility (owner decisions, 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15)
- **The decisions, verbatim:** "audit log entries that contain plan terms or adjustment reasons are visible only to roles that can see pay (view_sensitive or Owner). Add a hint under the adjustment reason field: 'Don't type amounts here.'" (2026-11-14). "Bonus reasons and pay-run exclusion reasons are hidden from anyone without pay access, the same as commission reasons. People with pay access see plan terms only within their own data scope; only the Owner sees every rep." (2026-11-15; "Owner answers, 2026-11-15").
- **Which entries:** `PAY_SENSITIVE_AUDIT_ACTIONS` in packages/shared (`permissions/pay-audit.ts`): "Added commission plan" and "Edited commission plan" (the terms: rates, tiers, target, override, draw, clawback), "Adjusted commission statement", "Accepted commission reversal" and "Waived commission reversal" (the reason) and, since 2026-11-15, "Added bonus", "Voided bonus" and "Left out of pay period" (the reason), with "Brought back into pay period", which the same audit call writes (its reason is null). The other commission and pay-run entries carry neither (approval, assignment, new version, moved assignment, cleared signature, pay-run steps) and stay readable to every audit viewer. A unit test (`apps/api/test/pay-audit-actions.test.ts`) reads the commission, pay-period and bonus services and the commission split writer (`setCommissionSplit`), and fails when an audit call that carries plan terms or a `reason` writes an action missing from the list; when any of their actions is in neither the list nor the test's "not pay-sensitive" list, or in both; when an action left readable has plan terms or a reason in its entry; or when an audit call's action is a variable outside the named pass-through helper (`auditPlan`). A new action is therefore always decided (deny by default). Every reason is now pay-sensitive: the test's exemption for bonus and exclusion reasons is gone.
- **Who sees the details** (`payAuditDetailsVisible`, per entry, on the request's effective access, not role names): the Owner sees every entry. Anyone else needs the pay `view_sensitive` key (`SENSITIVE.payRate`, `hr.employee.view_sensitive`; `canSeePayAudit` is this first gate), and then sees only entries about a person inside their data scope by the HR person rule (`seesPersonRecords`, hr.md "Data scope": `all` everyone, `branch` themselves and their direct reports, `own` themselves). The viewer's own employee is the live one their sign-in is linked to (`viewerEmployeeId`); an unlinked viewer sees nothing below `all`. An entry about no single person, the plan terms, needs scope `all` (safest: a plan's terms are the pay of everyone on it). No key, no details. So a pay-key holder at scope `all` other than the Owner, such as the default HR manager, reads every entry, every rep's and the plan terms; "only the Owner sees every rep" holds only against holders below scope `all` (the reading taken, "Owner answers, 2026-11-15").
- **Whom an entry is about** (`loadPayAuditSubjects`, apps/api `users/pay-audit-subjects.ts`, in a few batched queries, and only for a pay viewer below scope `all`, since everyone else reads all of these entries or none): a bonus entry by its bonus's employee (a voided bonus too); an exclusion by the `employeeId` in its after, else its before; a commission adjustment or reversal decision, which targets the adjustment row, by the employee that month's statement pays (`paidEmployee`: stored at approval, else the rep's sign-in link today); an entry that targets a statement by the employee it pays. The person rule reads that employee's manager today. An entry whose person can't be resolved, or is not a live employee, counts as company-wide, so only scope `all` reads it (safest).
- **What the others see (safest option that keeps the log honest):** the row stays (when, who, area, what, on whom), and each present before and after reads "Hidden: pay details" (`redactPayAudit`; an empty side stays empty). Leaving the row out would make the log look as if nothing happened and make counts differ between viewers; keeping it shows that a plan was changed or a statement adjusted, by whom, without the terms or the reason. `GET /audit` is the only read of audit entries the API serves; the screen's CSV export and row count are built from that answer, so they match.
- **The demo** redacts its audit list through the same shared rule (`demoAuditAsSeen` in `demo/audit-view.ts`, unit-tested in apps/web). Its entries name no person, so each counts as company-wide: a pay viewer below scope `all` who is not the Owner sees no pay details there, stricter than the API, never looser (safest). Among the default roles only the Owner and the HR manager hold the key, both at scope `all`, so only a custom role sees the difference.
- **Reason field:** the adjustment form says "Don't type amounts here." under Reason (the reason is shown on the statement and kept in the audit log). A reason is free text, so this is guidance, not a check.
- **Residual:** "Changed commission split" (an invoice's split percentages) and "Assigned commission plan" (the plan's name) are not plan terms and stay visible; free text outside these entries is outside the decisions. The person rule follows today's reporting line, so a manager who moves away stops reading a former report's entries and the new manager starts; a re-linked sign-in reads by its link today. ADR 0047's note that exclusion reasons reach every audit viewer no longer holds.

### Seed
Summit's three plans, four assignments with their signatures, 40 statements (all paid, ten pointing at the seeded run that paid them) and its adjustments (none in the fixtures) are seeded from the fixtures. Seeded history posts no journal entry. Since company migration 0005 the seed also writes one since-records-began manager history row per live employee, with the manager on the employee, and stores on each approved or paid statement the rep's linked employee, as the migration's backfill does, so a seeded company matches a migrated one; a second run adds nothing.

### Web
`commissionData` (one source for HR → Commissions), `listCommissionStatements`, `approveStatement`, `addCommissionAdjustment`, `decideReversal`, `saveCommissionPlan`, `assignPlan` and `teamCommission` go to the API in api mode. `acknowledgePlan`, `issueCreditNote` (already on the API) and My commission stay as they are; signing moves with the portal.

### First security review of PR #27
- **Margin base on split sales:** the first split member's plan sets an invoice's base for everyone in the split, so each line records the `basis` that produced its base, and override and reversal lines carry it on. `hideMarginBase` hides by that basis, not the line's own plan, so a paid revenue co-rep's share of a margin sale (and its override and reversals) shows no margin dollars. Credit-note reversals work out the fall in the base on that basis too.
- **Shaping, widened:** a "Changed commission split" audit entry on an invoice in the statement counts as setting its split, so setting and then clearing one no longer slips past; so does setting the manager (`managerId`, read from "Added employee" and "Edited employee" entries) of the statement's person or of anyone whose override is on it.
- **Managers paid overrides:** nobody changes a plan that pays (or would pay) an override while one of its people reports to them, nor puts their own people on such a plan (the Owner included: it is their own pay). Since 2026-11-15 "reports to them" means they managed one of those people on any day of the statements' window (`paysActorOverride`; "Overrides follow the manager on the line's day").
- **Team totals:** below scope `all`, only lines on invoices the viewer's scope covers count for other reps; the viewer's own lines count whole.
- **Carry:** each person's chain starts at their first line, adjustment or assignment, not at the window's edge, so a deficit or a recoverable draw leaving the twelve-month window still carries. No column stores the carry-out (no schema change in this task); it is derived from the lines and adjustments every time. A stored `carry_out` on `commission_statements`, frozen at approval, is a follow-up (STATUS); the owner's schema approval of 2026-11-15 did not cover it, so it stays one.
- **Adjustments:** dollars and cents, up to twelve digits, at most 1,000,000.00 either way (`MAX_ADJUSTMENT`).
- **Stranded statements:** the pay-period review lists approved, unpaid statements with something to pay whose rep is no longer linked, is a 1099 contractor or has left (`strandedCommission`); they are never marked paid by themselves. Since 2026-11-15 this is judged on the employee the statement pays, and a stored employee whose record was removed since is listed too ("The employee a statement pays").
- **Gross below commission:** a run is refused while someone's gross pay is below the commission it pays (an exempt full-day deduction larger than the salary), naming them and saying what to do (`commissionShortfalls`).
- **MFA:** every `hr.commission.*` key makes MFA mandatory, as the `hr.payroll.*` keys do.
- **Audit:** each assignment a new plan version ends, moves or creates gets its own "Moved commission assignment to new plan version" entry.
- **HR Home:** a 403 from `GET /commissions` reads as no statements, so the dashboard still loads.

### Second security review of PR #27
- **Self-made manager (blocking):** the manager of a person's linked employee is who is paid the override on their sales, so anyone who could edit People could make themselves someone's manager and collect it. Now only the Owner changes the manager of, or the sign-in linked to, someone with any assignment to a plan whose override is above zero (people.service.ts `checkOverrideManager`, now `checkCommissionPeople`, on add and edit). Approval is also refused, the Owner included, while a statement carries an override earned under a manager its own person set (`selfMadeManager`). Since the first review of PR #32 each override line is judged by its subject's manager history row in force on the company day the line was confirmed, the row that paid it, not by the latest change ("Self-made manager, judged on each line's day" below). Moving that person to another manager no longer clears the earlier days; the Owner clears them by setting that same manager again (move them to someone else and back, two edits, because re-saving the same manager records no change), which confirms the earlier days too.
- **Assignments checked at approval:** `checkApprover` now looks at the assignments of the statement's person and of every subject of its override lines, so whoever put a manager's people on an override plan can't approve the manager's statement.
- **Team totals switch:** `GET /commission-totals` also needs `hr.commissions` (404 when off, CLAUDE.md rule 13).
- **Margin through a basis change:** an invoice paid partly under a margin plan and partly under a paid revenue plan would show a revenue base beside a margin base, from which the cost follows. `hideMarginBase` now hides every line of an invoice that has any margin line.
- **Plan edited in place:** its assignments' signatures are cleared (each audited as "Cleared commission plan signature after plan edit", with no amount), so people sign the terms they are paid on, as a new version already required.
- **Stranded and payable lists:** a person can't be in both: the run pays only W-2, linked, not-terminated people (`payableEmployees`), and the stranded list holds only unlinked, 1099 or terminated ones, with statements not yet in a run. No change. Since 2026-11-15 both read the employee the statement pays (stored at approval, else today's link), not today's link: the run pays a stored employee who is W-2 and has not left, linked or not, and the stranded list holds statements with no such employee, a removed one, a 1099 contractor or someone who has left, so a statement is still never on both.

### Third security review of PR #27
- **Sign-in re-link (blocking):** commission is paid to the employee a person's sign-in is linked to, so an HR manager could unlink a rep and link the rep's sign-in to an ally's W-2 record, moving the rep's commission to the ally, and approve each step. Now only the Owner changes the sign-in link (link, unlink, or link another record to that user) of anyone with any commission assignment, past or future, or an approved statement not yet paid (people.service.ts `checkCommissionPeople`, on add and edit); the override-plan manager rule stays. Since the first review of PR #32 a paid statement counts too, and so does a record that manages (today or in its dated history) someone on a plan that pays an override (`hasCommissionHistory`, `managesOverridePeople`): override lines are worked out again on every read through today's links, so a re-link moves them out of approved and paid months as well (residual risks). "Added employee" and "Edited employee" entries always carry `userId` before and after. A link change counts as shaping: `checkApprover` refuses (the Owner exempt) an approver who changed the link of the statement's person or of anyone whose override is on it (`changedLinkOf`, from the person's latest approval by someone else, open-ended before one), and the pay run counts `userId` among the pay facts (`PAY_FACTS`), so whoever changed a person's link can't approve the run paying them. Since 2026-11-15 the employee is stored on the statement at approval and the run pays that employee ("The employee a statement pays"), so a re-link after approval no longer moves that statement's pay. The Owner-only link rule stays as it was, approved statements not yet paid included (the safest reading), because before approval a link still decides whom a statement will pay and whose override lines are whose.
- **Owner self-made manager:** nobody, the Owner included, makes their own employee the manager of someone on an override plan; otherwise the Owner's statement could never be approved. `selfMadeManager`'s refusal no longer says "Ask the Owner".
- **Race:** a People save takes `PAYROLL_LOCK` first (order `PAYROLL_LOCK` → employee → `user_profiles`), so a manager or link change can't interleave with a statement or pay-run approval. No path takes an employee lock and then `PAYROLL_LOCK`.
- **Team totals module:** `GET /commission-totals` also needs the `hr` module (404 without it), as the rest of commissions does.

### Overrides follow the manager on the line's day (owner decision, 2026-11-15)
- **Table:** `employee_manager_history` (company migration 0005): employee, manager (null: none) and `effective_from` (a company day; null: since records began), with an index on `(employee_id, effective_from)`. Append-only by convention, as `time_off_entries` is, with no trigger. `employees.manager_id` stays the current manager. docs/data-model.md, "HR".
- **Writes:** a People add or edit that sets or changes a manager appends a row in the save's transaction, after `PAYROLL_LOCK` and the employee lock and before `user_profiles` (`recordManager` in people.service.ts). The change takes effect on the company's day, by the database clock read once the locks are held (ADR 0047). Nothing back-dates it, so the days before keep their manager (safest: a back-dated reorg would move override lines that were already worked out). A person added with a manager gets a since-records-began row, as nothing about them is earlier. A person with no row yet (added without a manager, or outside the app) first gets a since-records-began row with the manager they had, then the dated row. Several changes on one day each add a row, and the last recorded one wins.
- **Reads:** `managerOn` (packages/shared `hr/manager-history.ts`) takes the row with the latest `effective_from` on or before the day; a since-records-began row comes before every dated one, and on a tie the later recorded row (by its UUID v7 id) wins. A day before an employee's first row reads that first row. An employee with no row reads today's `manager_id`, as before the table. `CommissionInputs.managerOf(userId, at)` is called with the moment the line's payment (its review, else its recording) or applied credit was confirmed (`lineConfirmedAt`), and `loadManagerOf` goes user → today's linked employee → their manager on the company's day of that moment → that manager's user today. Manager changes are dated on the company's calendar, so the lookup is too: a payment confirmed on a company's evening, already the next day in UTC, keeps the manager of the company's day it happened (first review of PR #32). The line's `confirmedOn`, month and windows are still its UTC day (STATUS item on payment days read as UTC). A reversal copies its line's `userId`, so a later reorg never moves a reversal to the new manager. The demo keeps no history and reads today's manager for every day (`commission-actions.ts`).
- **Backfill (safest):** migration 0005 gives every live employee one since-records-began row with their manager at the time, the manager overrides were worked out with until then, so no override already worked out moves. `created_by` is the employee row's; ids are UUID v7 made in plain SQL (CLAUDE.md rule 11). The seed writes the same rows.
- **Never one's own override:** `paysActorOverride` (plan edits and assignments; "Managers paid overrides" above) now counts anyone who managed one of those people on any day from the first of the oldest month a statement shows (`last12Months`) through today, a manager overtaken later the same day included (`loadManagersBetween`), not only today's manager, because a past manager may still be paid an override on those months (safest).
- **Unchanged:** only the Owner changes the manager of someone on a plan with an override, any assignment counting, past or future; the approver shaping checks (`setManagerOf`, `changedLinkOf`) read the "Added employee" and "Edited employee" entries as before.
- **Self-made manager, judged on each line's day (first review of PR #32, blocking):** with dated managers the latest change no longer decides who is paid for earlier days, so `selfMadeManager` no longer reads it. For each override line on the statement it takes the subject's history row in force on the company day of the line's confirmed moment, the same row `loadManagerOf` paid it by (`managerRowOn`), and refuses when that row's manager was set by the statement's own person and nobody else has set that same manager again since (`managerSelfSetOn` in packages/shared; `loadSelfSetManager`). Who set a row: a dated row, whoever saved it (`created_by`, written by `recordManager`); a since-records-began row stands for the manager the person had before their history began, so whoever made the last manager change in the "Added employee" and "Edited employee" entries before the person's first dated row was written (with no dated row, the last one of all); its own `created_by` (the backfill's, or the first editor's) is not read. "Set again" means a later row naming the same manager, by someone else, that was in force on some day (a row overtaken the same day doesn't count). A person with no history rows falls back to the latest change in the audit entries, as their overrides follow today's manager. Example: HR user M makes themselves Rep R's manager while R is on no override plan, R later joins one, and the Owner moves R to X; M's earlier months still carry R's overrides and stay refused, whoever approves, until someone else sets M as R's manager again.

### The employee a statement pays (owner decision, 2026-11-15)
- **Column:** `commission_statements.employee_id` (uuid, nullable, foreign key to `employees`; company migration 0005). Approval stores the rep's linked live employee at that moment (`payrollHome`) whenever there is one, even with nothing to pay; it is null on a draft and on a statement approved while the rep had no link. No response carries it: `CommissionStatement` and `GET /commissions` are unchanged.
- **Paying:** `paidEmployee` is the stored employee, else, for a statement stored without one, the rep's sign-in link today. `commissionForRun` picks statements by that employee, so a re-link after approval never moves the pay. Pay-run approval links the statements and export marks them paid, as before.
- **Stranded statements** (`strandedCommission`) are judged on the same employee, so the review never lists a statement the next run pays: there is none ("Their sign-in is not linked to an employee."), its record was removed since ("Their employee record was removed, so no pay run includes them.", new), it is a 1099 contractor, or they have left.
- **Backfill:** migration 0005 stores on every approved or paid statement the rep's live link at the time, the employee runs paid before the column, so nothing already worked out moves; drafts stay null and get their employee when approved. The seed does the same.

## Residual risks
- **Manager history dates the manager, not the sign-in links:** an override goes to the user linked today to the manager on the line's day, and the rep is found by today's link. Override lines are worked out again on every read, and an approved or paid statement keeps only its posted payable, not its lines, so a re-link of the rep or of the manager moves override lines of every month, approved and paid months included, to another user's open statement for that month, which can then be approved and paid a second time. Only the Owner may make such a re-link (`hasCommissionHistory`, since the first review of PR #32 also counting paid statements and records that manage someone on an override plan), so the remaining case is an Owner re-link, for example a rehire on a new record. Making override attribution stable once a month is approved (storing each approved statement's lines or recipients, or dated sign-in links) needs a schema change the owner has not approved: a follow-up and owner question. A past manager whose employee record was removed, or who has no sign-in today, is paid no override, as with today's manager before.
- **A self-made override can't be dropped in the app:** a month carrying an override earned under a manager its own person set is refused until someone else sets that same manager again; nothing in the app removes such a line, and months are approved in order, so that person's later months wait too. The Owner either confirms the manager (setting them again) or settles outside the app; an owner question asks whether a way to waive such lines is wanted.
- **Days on two calendars:** manager changes and the manager lookup use the company's day; a line's `confirmedOn`, month, clawback window and the "not after today" cut still use the UTC day of the payment's moment (STATUS item on payment `recordedAt`/`voidedAt` read as UTC). A payment confirmed on a company's evening therefore keeps the old manager across a reorg the next morning, but sits in the next UTC day's month. Same-day changes are ordered by UUID v7 ids made in the app; with several API servers, clocks a millisecond apart could swap two, which `PAYROLL_LOCK` makes very unlikely. Nothing in the database stops an UPDATE or DELETE on the history (no trigger, unlike `audit_log`).
- **Overtime true-up allocation (question for the owner and accountant):** the run pays the posted amount of each statement, but the true-up allocates the statements' lines, worked out again, to workweeks (`commissionAllocations`). If lines drifted after approval, or adjustments and the draw make the posted payable differ from the lines' total, the regular-rate true-up uses the lines, not what was paid. Which figure the regular rate should use is for the accountant.
- **Ally as manager:** linking a sign-in to an employee who manages people on override plans, or a manager colluding with an Owner-approved reorg, is not caught beyond the link rule (scope `all` or the Owner) and the audit log.
- **Gross clamp at zero:** gross pay never goes below zero, so in a run approved with gross below its commission part of the commission would stay in Accrued Payroll unpaid. Approval now refuses such a run (above); the remaining risk is a run settled outside the app.
- **Derived carry:** until carry-out is stored (not approved on 2026-11-15), an approved month's carry is worked out again from live lines; drift after approval (below) could move a later month's carry-in.
- **Margin is still derivable:** the base is hidden, but commission and the plan's rate are shown, so on a one-tier margin plan base = commission ÷ rate, and with `collected` the margin percent follows. Hiding commission from HR would break their job; a question for the owner.
- **A statement's payable shows to ledger readers:** one Commissions line per statement at the rep's home location. Ledger readers (Owner, Accountant) hold `hr.payroll.view` and see the commission on pay lines anyway.
- **Draws are expensed when approved:** a recoverable draw paid in a short month is booked as commission expense, not as an advance (asset) recovered later. The accountant may prefer an advance account.
- **Postings are dated the month's end:** a statement can't be approved into a closed accounting period (409 `period_closed`); approve before closing the month, or reopen the books.
- **Strict maker-checker:** whoever added a plan can never approve statements under it, so a company needs two HR approvers (or the Owner) per plan's people. Accepted as the task asks.
- **Seeded decisions:** the fixtures' reversal decisions (none today) would name demo line ids, which the API's ids (seed UUIDs) don't match.
- **Drift after approval:** confirmations, voids and credit notes land in the month they happen (rules 2, 9 and 10) and back-dated assignments are refused, so an approved month's lines should not move; if a figure still drifts (say an edited split before the first confirmed payment of an older invoice), the posted amount is what is shown and paid, and the difference is not trued up automatically.
- **Collusion** still defeats separation of duties (ADR 0046).

## Consequences
- Commissions (6060) appears in the chart; LedgerService creates it on first use in existing companies.
- Pay runs on the API pay commission; the approval of a run with waiting statements no longer fails.
- The employee portal (My commission, signing) is the next task; until then `acknowledgePlan` stays on the demo client.
- Company migration 0005 adds `employee_manager_history` and `commission_statements.employee_id` and backfills both, so every company migrated from 0004 keeps the overrides and pay it had (2026-11-15).
