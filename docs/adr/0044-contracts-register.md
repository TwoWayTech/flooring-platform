# ADR 0044: Contracts part 1 on the API (register, change orders, waivers, insurance)

- **Status:** accepted, 2026-11-14
- **Context:** contracts.md describes the demo's contracts: drafts with a schedule of values, sending, a simulated signature, completion, closing, termination, renewal, change orders, lien waivers and certificates of insurance, plus progress billings and retainage releases that raise invoices and bills. The schema already exists (`packages/db/src/company/contracts.ts`, ADR 0026). Billings post to the ledger through invoices and bills, so they come in part 2 with their postings.

## Decision
- **Routes** (`modules/contracts`, module `contracts`): reads `GET /contracts`, `/contract-lines`, `/change-orders` (`contracts.contract.view`), `GET /contract-billings` (`contracts.billing.view`, read only), `GET /lien-waivers`, `/insurance-certificates` (`contracts.compliance.view`). Writes `POST /contracts` (`create`), `PUT /contracts/:id` (`edit`, draft only), `POST /contracts/:id/send`, `/recall`, `/complete`, `/close`, `/files` (`edit`), `/sign`, `/terminate` (`approve`), `/renew` (`create`), `POST /contracts/:id/change-orders` (`contracts.change_order.create`), `POST /change-orders/:id/decision` (`contracts.change_order.approve`), `POST /contracts/:id/lien-waivers` and `PUT /contracts/:id/insurance-certificate` (`contracts.compliance.manage`). Keys are exactly the spec's "Who uses it" table.
- **Feature switches:** every route needs `contracts.register`; billings also need `contracts.billing`, waivers and insurance also `contracts.compliance`. Switched off, they answer 404 (CLAUDE.md rule 13). The demo read neither of the two smaller switches (spec edge case 20).
- **Data scope** (`byRep`): own by `salesRepId`, branch by `branchId`, all. Lines, change orders, billings, waivers and certificates follow their contract, including waivers and insurance, which the demo did not filter. Reads filter; writes on a contract outside scope answer 404. Contract files at `GET /files/:id` need `contracts.contract.view` and the contract's scope. The signature's hash covers every signed term (lines' rates and milestone dates included, estimated cost excluded), and its version counts the sends.
- **New contracts:** the rep is always the creator and the branch the creator's; someone with no branch may pick one only with scope `all`, otherwise the save is refused. An edit keeps the rep and branch, so nobody moves a contract out of anyone's scope. The customer must be in the creator's scope; a subcontractor must be an active supplier; the warehouse must exist. A master stores value and retainage 0 and no lines; a project's original value is the sum of its lines.
- **Estimated cost** (`contracts.contract.view_sensitive`, already in `SENSITIVE_FIELDS`): stripped from every response without the key. A save takes it from the request only from a holder who sent one; otherwise each line keeps the stored cost of the line with the same code (or zero), which fixes spec edge case 13.
- **Signature:** simulated as in the demo. The `SignatureRecord` is stored on the contract with the typed signer, the recorder's name and the times. **No PDF is generated or filed on the API**; instead `hash` is a SHA-256 of the signed terms and schedule, so a later change is detectable. `consentVersion` stays `demo-1`.
- **Close** (rule 18): held from posted billings (`contractFigures`); the balance is each posted billing's invoice ledger (`invoiceState`) and, unlike the demo (edge case 2), its bill's ledger too; then a received final waiver. An override needs `contracts.contract.approve` (403 otherwise) and a reason of at least 3 characters, and is stored only when a check failed.
- **Renew:** a real new draft (fixing edge case 1): new number, same rep, branch, terms and current lines (with estimated cost), dates moved on by the old term's length, `renewedFromId` set, no signature, files or milestone dates. Only a signed contract (not draft or sent) renews.
- **Stricter guards:** Mark completed is refused on a master; change orders and lien waivers are refused on a master; change orders are approved only while the contract is active or completed; a change-order row may only name a line of its own contract; rows on one line add up before the check and the change (PR #23 review); a new line needs an amount above zero; change orders sit under the `contracts.billing` switch, as features.ts names it; a waiver's payment must be the contract party's own (customer payment on a customer contract, supplier payment on a subcontract); a coverage may not expire before it takes effect.
- **Numbers:** `CTR-` from 1001, highest plus one, under a company-wide advisory lock. Change orders are `CO-<count + 1>` per contract, taken under the contract's row lock.
- **Notifications:** sending raises `contract.signature` to the other party through NotificationService, versioned by the send time, so a send after a recall is a new message. A failure to queue never undoes the send.
- **Audit:** every write records the spec's action string with target `<number>, <title>` in the same transaction.
- **Files:** contract documents use the proof-file path (ADR 0038): base64 in the body, at most 3 per contract, owner type `contract`; `POST /contracts/:id/files` takes the proof body size.
- **Web:** the contract list, contract page and dashboard widget read `contractsData()` (contracts, lines, change orders, billings, waivers, certificates, customers, suppliers, warehouses, bills, supplier payments) so every id comes from one source.

## Consequences
- In api mode, posting a billing, releasing retainage and voiding a billing still go to the demo and fail with "This contract no longer exists." until part 2 moves them.
- Summit's seed has contracts, lines, change orders, the waiver that points at no unseeded payment, and the certificate. Billings, contract invoices, subcontract bills, the waiver on a contract-invoice payment, and the signed PDFs (they have no bytes) wait for part 2.
- Seeded and API-signed contracts carry no signed PDF on the API.
- No separation of duties between submitting and approving a change order (edge case 19), as in the demo; an open question for the owner.
