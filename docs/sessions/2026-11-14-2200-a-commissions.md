## Safest options taken (ADR 0048, blueprint-delta)
- **Scope:** company-wide only, like payroll; the rep's own view waits for the portal.
- **Frozen months:** months approved in order; nothing changes a month up to the last approved one; the approved payable is what posted.
- **Plans:** versioned once any approved statement exists from the plan's start (the demo's check missed managers' overrides).
- **Own and shaped:** own statement, plan or assignment refused for everyone, the Owner included; shaping (adjustment, decision, assignment, plan, sale split) refused unless the Owner.
- **Payroll:** a paying statement needs the rep linked to a W-2 employee; commission accrues once (Commissions 6060) and the run never posts it to Wages.
- **Margin:** the base of margin-plan lines is hidden without `sales.margin.view_sensitive`; invoice cost never leaves the server.
