## Plan
Sales is the largest module, so it lands in two pull requests.
1. **Part 1 (this branch):**
   - **Customers:** addresses, contacts and certificates, read with data scope (own, branch or all, spec "Data scope").
   - **Quotes:** rooms and lines. The server computes totals with the shared `computeTotals` and the tax engine (`taxContextFor`, `resolveTaxRule`, `withOverride`) from the company's own tax rules, county rates and company default. A tax override needs `sales.tax.override`, and a waste change needs `sales.waste.view`. Send, accept and decline follow the shared quote rules, and quote numbers are taken under a lock.
   - **Margins and cost:** stripped by name on the server.
   - **Seed:** customers and quotes.
   - **Web:** `salesData()` and the quote and customer methods.
2. **Part 2 (next branch):** invoices (conversion, sent, void, credit notes), reservations and the cut or issue (inventory part 2), payments with the two-person check, write-offs, credits and refunds.

**Accountant decisions** (STATUS): the states of operation and tax registrations stay company settings. The safe default is to collect only where a rule exists.
