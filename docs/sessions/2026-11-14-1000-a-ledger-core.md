P12 lands in three parts, like sales:

1. **This part (ADR 0039):**
   - **Schema (company migration 0002):**
     - `ledger_accounts`, a chart of accounts with stable keys, editable names and the default accounts;
     - `journal_entries` and `journal_lines`, both append-only;
     - `period_closes`, append-only, where the latest row sets the closed-through date.
   - **Database triggers:**
     - each entry must balance at commit;
     - lines are one-sided and not negative;
     - no entry may be dated on or before the closed-through date;
     - no update or delete.
   - **LedgerService** (`core/ledger`): the default accounts are made on first use; it posts a balanced entry in the caller's transaction, reverses an entry on the void date, and refuses closed periods.
   - **Sales postings** replace the `requireLedger` gate, with the posting rules in packages/shared:
     - invoice: AR against sales and sales tax;
     - confirmed payment: cash against AR, with any extra to customer credit;
     - write-off: bad debt against AR;
     - credit note: sales returns and sales tax against customer credit;
     - apply credit: customer credit against AR;
     - refund: customer credit against cash;
     - voids: the reversing entry.
   - **Credit-note decision (STATUS, PR #15 review), safe default:** a new credit note settles its own invoice's open balance first. Only the rest becomes customer credit, so cash can never be refunded against money that was never collected.
   - **Reads:** `GET /ledger/trial-balance` with `reports.financial.view`; close a period with `settings.company.manage`, audited.
   - **Tests:**
     - unit tests for each posting;
     - a randomised sequence test: debits equal credits and the trial balance is zero;
     - API tests: AR matches the open invoice balances, closed periods are refused, and reversals post.
2. **Part 2:** expenses on the API (finance-expenses), with receipts, approval, pay later, recurring drafts and their postings.
3. **Part 3:**
