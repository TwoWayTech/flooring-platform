### Key decisions (a) to (h)
- **(a) Data scope.** Narrowing the Business report to a branch fails: shared costs spread by company-wide weights give away company revenue; most sections have no location; a branch manager could work out one person's pay from Direct expenses at a small showroom; and the spec has no branch version. So the Business report and P&L need data scope `all` (403 "This report covers the whole company."), and are hidden on screen and refused by the demo for anyone else. Sales by category and Payments received apply data scope on the server with the sales routes' rule (own or branch, `scopeAllows`); customer names show only for customers the person may see. The Manager loses the Business report and P&L until the owner answers question 1.
- **(b) Stripping on the server.** Four flags from the effective permissions and one data fact: `margin` = `sales.margin.view_sensitive` and `inventory.product.view_sensitive` and (`contracts.contract.view_sensitive` or no live contract invoice); `payroll` = `hr.payroll.view`; `people` = `payroll` and `hr.employee.view_sensitive`; `stock` = `inventory.product.view_sensitive`. Hidden figures are never computed, so they are absent from rows, values, totals and drill records, not zero. The global interceptor stays as a second net and never blanks a figure the flags allow.
- **(c) One calculation.** The API and the demo run the same shared functions. API readers load only the columns and dates `businessWindow` names (a period of at most 366 days plus the comparison period; balances read narrow history columns). Each request reads in one `READ ONLY`, `REPEATABLE READ` transaction. Drill lists are capped at 200 per row, newest first, with a `recordCount`.
- **(d) Exports.** CSV and PDF stay in the browser, built from the API's answer. The buttons follow the server's `exports` list. No export route; any future server export (Excel, emailed reports) must check the export key on the server.
- **(e) Switches.** `reports.hub` off: every route answers 404 and the page shows no reports. A single report's switch off: that route answers 404 and the options leave it out. The four removed reports get no route and are no longer listed; their web builders stay (rule 25). `features.ts` and FEATURES.md do not change.
- **(f) Product and module.** `@RequireProduct("flooring")` and `@RequireModule("core")` on the controller: an HR Suite company gets 403 `product_not_enabled`, a company with `core` off 403 `module_not_enabled`.
- **(g) The ledger and the gate.** Reports come from the documents, not the ledger, as the spec says (seeded history posted nothing; reconciliation belongs to the whole-product check). The gate is proven by a shared property test, an API integration test on Summit plus edge rows, equality between the API and the shared function, and an api-mode e2e check on screen and in the CSV.
- **(h) Performance.** About 16 narrow queries in one transaction for the Business report on Summit; memoised lookups in the shared code; decimal sorting; stock read only with the `stock` flag as one grouped sum. CI gains one integration file, one api-mode spec and a few demo e2e lines. Missing date indexes are a migration, deferred to the owner.

## Decisions and why
Each goes into ADR 0055 "Safest options taken", the blueprint-delta row and the spec's "Real build" section.

1. **S1. Business report and P&L need data scope `all`.** Anyone else gets 403 "This report covers the whole company.", and the screen and the demo hide or refuse them too. Why: (a) above; this also closes the spec's known limits "Data scope differs between reports" and "Profit and loss mixes scopes".
2. **S2. Sales by category and Payments received apply data scope on the server,** with the invoice and customer rules of the sales routes (own or branch). Why: the same rows a person may already list through `GET /invoices` and `GET /payments`.
3. **S3. Pay sight is `hr.payroll.view`;** People costs also need `hr.employee.view_sensitive`. Without pay sight, payroll expenses and pay periods are dropped before any figure is computed (the API does not read them), people costs are left out of Operating expenses, net profit and Profit by location, and the labels and notes say so ("Operating expenses, excluding people costs", "Net profit, before people costs and income tax", "Expenses, excluding payroll", "Net income, before payroll"). Why: the rule `GET /expenses` and `GET /pay-periods` already use (ADR 0041), so pay can't be worked out from a report. An Owner of a company with HR off loses pay sight.
4. **S4. Cost-of-goods figures also need `contracts.contract.view_sensitive` while the company has a live contract invoice,** whatever the module state. Why: a contract invoice's cost is the estimated cost share that the sales routes strip without that key. With Contracts off nobody sees these figures while contract invoices exist; no default role is affected while Contracts is on.
5. **S5. Purchases by supplier needs `inventory.product.view_sensitive`.** Why: it adds up order unit costs, which purchasing strips without that key.
6. **S6. Sales by category's Cost and Margin need the full `margin` flag** (the spec named only the product-cost key), and hidden columns are absent from rows and totals. Why: margin is revenue less cost; the demo kept them in the totals.
7. **S7. Visibility is stripped at the source,** and the expense sections' column id moves from `cost` to `amount`. Why: hidden figures can't leak through values, totals or drill records, and the interceptor's reserved name is never used for a figure that isn't sensitive.
8. **S8. Bounds.** A report day is a real `yyyy-MM-dd` with a year from 1900 to 2999; the Business report period is at most 366 days (a longer one is a 400); the hub reports take only their four presets; drill lists are capped at 200 per row and say "Showing the latest 200 of N records.". Why: bounded reads, no broken date maths, and no silently cut lists.
9. **S9. One read-only snapshot per request** (`READ ONLY`, `REPEATABLE READ`). Why: every figure comes from the same moment, so the location rows can't disagree with the total; a write inside it is refused.
10. **S10. Strict queries.** An unknown or repeated parameter is a 400; ids and categories are checked against what exists; locations must be live (warehouses for the hub reports); `?companyId=` is an unknown parameter. Why: deny by default, and the tenant only ever comes from the session.
11. **S11. `Cache-Control: private, no-store`** on every report answer. Why: financial figures should not sit in shared or browser caches.
12. **S12. Write-off `at` and goods receipt `receivedAt` are read as company days** in the company's time zone. Why: closes the STATUS follow-up for reports; a correctness fix more than a safety one.
13. **S13. Flooring Suite only, module `core`.** Why: the reports belong to the Flooring product; HR Suite and a company with `core` off are refused before the permission check.
14. **S14. The removed reports have no route** and the page no longer lists them; bringing one back needs an API route first. Why: a switched-off feature is hidden everywhere, the API included.
15. **S15. No export route;** the buttons follow the server's `exports`. Why: the data a person may view is already in their browser; any future server export must check the export key on the server.
16. **S16. DemoStore follows S1 to S18** with the same shared functions, messages, scope rule and empty options; a demo View as Manager no longer sees the Business report or P&L. Why: one behaviour in both modes.
17. **S17. Every cost-of-goods figure also needs `inventory.product.view_sensitive`.** Why: blueprint p27, "Reports showing cost or margin require the matching permission", and `GET /invoices` strips invoice cost without it. No default role is affected.
18. **S18. The P&L's Cost of goods, Gross margin, Margin and Net income need the `margin` flag;** without it the P&L shows Month, Revenue and Expenses. Why: the same rule as the Business report; the demo showed them to every holder of `reports.financial.view`. No default role is affected.

## For the owner
Also in ADR 0055 "For the owner".

1. **Branch managers (S1).** Should a Manager get a branch Business report and P&L? It needs a spec: which sections, shared costs as one line with no weights, and no company-wide balances, cash, taxes or people costs. Until then they are refused on the API and in the demo.
2. **Pay sight (S3).** Pay-inclusive figures now need `hr.payroll.view`, the Owner included, so with HR off nobody sees people costs. Right? And without pay sight, should Operating expenses and net profit show with people costs left out and labelled, as built, or should the Summary stop at gross profit?
3. **Contracts (S4).** While live contract invoices exist, cost-of-goods figures need `contracts.contract.view_sensitive`; with Contracts off nobody, the Owner included, sees them. Accept, or should the Owner keep them?
4. **Cost key (S17) and the P&L (S18).** Cost-of-goods figures also need `inventory.product.view_sensitive`, and the P&L hides its cost and margin columns without the `margin` flag. No default role changes. Confirm.
5. **Period length (S8).** Is a 366-day cap on the Business report period right, or should it allow up to two years for year-over-year?
6. **Exports (S15).** Exports stay screen-checked because viewing already sends the data. Acceptable until Excel and emailed reports come, when the server will check the export key?
7. **Trial balance.** `GET /ledger/trial-balance` shows the Cost of Goods Sold and Wages accounts to anyone with `reports.financial.view` and scope `all`, without the margin, cost or payroll keys. Should P13 gate those accounts the same way as the reports?
8. **Schema follow-ups** (need a migration): freezing shared-cost shares when a period closes (`expense_allocations`, ADR 0023), and indexes on `invoices.issued_at`, `expenses.date` and `payments_received.date`.
9. **Removed reports (S14).** Keep their web builders as rule 25 says, or delete them as dead code now that the page no longer lists them?
