## Plan
HR lands in two parts:
1. **This part (ADR 0046):** people (employees, onboarding checklist, documents, offboarding), timesheets with clock entries and overtime, and time-off policies, balances and requests on the API, gated by the `hr` module so Flooring Suite and HR Suite companies share the routes.
2. **Part 2:** pay periods, bonuses and contractor payments, with their payroll postings.

The build was handed to a general-purpose subagent with a full brief; tests and reviews follow.

## What was built
- `apps/api/src/modules/hr/`: records, people, timesheets and time-off services, controllers;
- new shared rules in `packages/shared/src/hr`, which the demo now uses too;
- the HR seed (`packages/db/src/seed/hr.ts`);
- `hrData()` for the HR screens;
- ADR 0046, the API contract and blueprint-delta.

Key decisions:
- **Pay rate:** stripped without `hr.employee.view_sensitive`, kept on saves by others, never changed by its owner, never in audit.
- **Data scope** (`ownOrReports`): own, or own plus direct reports, or all.
- **Separation of duties:** nobody decides their own timesheet or time off.
- **Validation:** strict Zod refuses any Social Security or bank field.
- **Files:** `GET /files/:id` asks the route for the `platform` module, and each file type checks its own module.

## Reviews
First security review: 2 blocking (submitted-week upload; self-guards failing open for unlinked sign-ins), fixed; should-fix items 3–9 done.
1. Upload over a submitted week: the shared timesheet resolver refuses submitted weeks, future weeks, people who don't use timesheets and bad hours; the API checks each written week again under the locks.
2. Self-guards for unlinked sign-ins: `selfCheck` treats an employee as one's own by the sign-in link or the verified email, for refusals only (pay, decisions, reopen, balance adjustments, own-hours edits).
3. Own pay facts: nobody changes their own pay type, worker type, FLSA status or salaried hours either.
4. Notifications: approvers are chosen by effective permissions (`AccessService.membersAccess`) and a data scope that covers the employee.
5. Deadlock: batch decisions lock employees in id order, then sheets (`lockTimesheets`); the upload locks sheets in id order after the employees.
6. Clock-out: refused when the day would pass 24 hours.
7. Without view_sensitive the stored pay type and salaried hours stay, like the rate.
8. Hours as decimal strings on the API (`TimesheetWire`); the web client converts at the ApiClient boundary (ADR 0046).
9. An approver's upload refuses their own rows without `hr.timesheet.edit`, and nobody reopens their own week.

Tests added: 7 in `hr.int.test.ts` (25 now) and 1 shared import test. Re-run: typecheck, lint, `pnpm test` (shared 401, api 56, web 13, db 11, providers 11), hr + permissions + isolation integration 65 of 65, `seed.int.test.ts` 11 of 11.

Second security review: 1 blocking (email self-match could be switched off by editing one's own email), fixed; should-fix 2–5 and notes 6–7 done.
1. Email self-match: `PeopleService.save` always runs `selfCheck`; the email on one's own record can't change, and one's own sign-in email can't go on another record (add or edit).
2. Manager changes: only data scope `all` sets or changes `managerId` (400 otherwise).
3. Own record: no status or commission-visibility change, no own checklist ticks, no own offboarding.
4. Clock: clock-in refused for someone who has left; both clock routes need `hr.timesheets` too.
5. Time-off approval re-runs `timeOffRequestProblem` under the employee lock, leaving that request out of pending.
6. Approver notices also leave out a member whose sign-in email is the employee's.
7. A saver without `hr.employee.view` keeps the stored email, phone and emergency contact, and gets work facts only back (only custom roles can be such a saver).

Tests added: 7 in `hr.int.test.ts` (32 now).
