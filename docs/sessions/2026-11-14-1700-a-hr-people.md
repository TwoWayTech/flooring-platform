## Plan
HR lands in two parts:
1. **This part (ADR 0046):** people (employees, onboarding checklist, documents, offboarding), timesheets with clock entries and overtime, and time-off policies, balances and requests on the API, gated by the `hr` module so Flooring Suite and HR Suite companies share the routes.
2. **Part 2:** pay periods, bonuses and contractor payments, with their payroll postings.

The build was handed to a general-purpose subagent with a full brief; tests and reviews follow.

## What was built
- `apps/api/src/modules/hr/`: records, people, timesheets and time-off services, controllers;
- new shared rules in `packages/shared/src/hr`, which the demo now uses too;
- the HR seed (`packages/db/src/seed/hr.ts`);
- `hrData()` for the HR screens;
- ADR 0046, the API contract and blueprint-delta.

Key decisions:
- **Pay rate:** stripped without `hr.employee.view_sensitive`, kept on saves by others, never changed by its owner, never in audit.
- **Data scope** (`ownOrReports`): own, or own plus direct reports, or all.
- **Separation of duties:** nobody decides their own timesheet or time off.
- **Validation:** strict Zod refuses any Social Security or bank field.
- **Files:** `GET /files/:id` asks the route for the `platform` module, and each file type checks its own module.
