  - A sweeper that queues `queued` rows with no job: queueing failed, or the company was read-only when the worker ran.
  - Validate the sender settings with Zod when Settings → Email saves them.
  - The bounce webhook (`bounced`).
  - Attachments through StorageProvider.
  - Business events, raised from their modules.
- **Concurrent provisioning of one slug:** two runs at once can both pass the resume gate. The final update no longer overwrites another status, but the second run could still re-seed. The fix is to claim the row (a conditional update to a `seeding` status) or take an advisory lock on the tenant id (PR #8 re-review). Running provisioning twice at once is an operator error today.
- **move-tenant:** maintenance mode, pg_dump/pg_restore, checksums, the connection swap, and `TenantConnectionService.invalidate(companyId)` (ADR 0030).
- **TenantConnectionService hardening (PR #6 third review, none blocking):**
  - **Leases:** count users per pool (a `withDb(tenant, fn)` wrapper or acquire and release), so the over-cap close and eviction never end a pool that a request is still using. Today that can happen once more than 2 × `TENANT_POOL_CACHE` companies are active within 30 s.
  - **A real connection bound:** bound the total connections rather than the pool count, pass `end({ timeout })`, and lower the `TENANT_POOL_CACHE` maximum (500 × 3 × 2 is far above PostgreSQL's 100).
  - **Shutdown:** await the opens still in flight before draining, so none leaks.
  - **Tests:** prove one pool per company, that parallel first requests share one open, and eviction while an open is in flight.

## Blockers and open questions
- **Accountant questions (ledger, ADR 0039):**
  - Is the default chart of accounts right, and which accounts should be added or renamed? **Default chosen, awaiting accountant:** the default chart.
  - Which costing method for stock and cost of goods? **Default chosen, awaiting accountant:** average cost, as a company setting.
  - Should sales tax be reported on the accrual basis (posted when invoiced), as now, or the cash basis? **Default chosen, awaiting accountant:** accrual, with cash basis available as a company setting.
  - Should one Cash and Bank account be split by bank account or payment method? **Default chosen, awaiting accountant:** split by bank account.
- **Accountant questions (sales tax, ADR 0037):** which states is the client registered to collect sales tax in, and what are its states of operation? Are the seeded rates and per-category taxability (materials taxed; separately stated labor and delivery not) right for each state? Until answered, tax is collected only where a rule exists for the job site, or the company default in its states of operation.
